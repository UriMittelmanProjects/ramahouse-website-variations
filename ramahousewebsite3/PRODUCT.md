# Rama House website

Rama House is a family-owned Thai restaurant in Bothell, Washington. The site helps local guests review menus, order pickup, find the dining room, and understand the restaurant before visiting.

## Primary guests

Local diners choosing lunch, dinner, or takeout. The experience should work quickly on phones and remain expressive on larger screens.

## Core actions

- Review lunch and dinner menus.
- Start an online pickup order.
- Find the address, hours, and phone number.
- See the food and dining room.

## Voice

Copy is direct, warm, specific, and brief. It avoids rhetorical questions, contrast formulas, fragmented lists, and exaggerated claims.
