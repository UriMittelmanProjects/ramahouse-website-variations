# Design direction

## Mode

Experience mode with playful product movement inspired by modern food and beverage sites.

## Palette

- Ink: #080b09
- Forest: #123c2c
- Lime: #d8f4a7
- Orange: #ff6331
- Paper: #f3f1e8
- White: #ffffff

## Typography

Fraunces carries large display lines. DM Sans handles navigation, descriptions, and interface text. All text uses regular roman styling with zero letter spacing.

## Composition

Full-width color fields provide structure. Transparent drink and plate images form the main visual language. Sections stay unframed, while repeated menu items may use compact cards.

## Motion

Motion supports reading order and reveals food as the page progresses. Scroll-linked movement uses spring smoothing. Reduced-motion preferences remove parallax, looping movement, and large transitions.

## Constraints

Avoid gradients, glass effects, decorative blobs, nested cards, large rounded containers, and negative letter spacing. Keep controls stable and touch targets at least 44 pixels high.
